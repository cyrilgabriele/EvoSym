\subsection{Use cases and where language belongs}
\label{sec:usecases}

The domain expert's mandate was to decide where free language enters and leaves the system, which facts must come from the knowledge base, and what a wrong answer costs the passenger or SBB~\citep{h2task}. In FlightMode the team applied these questions to four SBB use cases. Automatic delay compensation (U2) needs no model, because its inputs are structured train data; rules alone suffice. A station chat (U1) does: passengers write in several languages and with names such as ``Züri'' that match no station, while the model alone cannot hold the knowledge base or ground its answers in it.

The refund assistant follows the same split. Language enters as the passenger's message and leaves as the reply; the station, train, arrival times, delay, percentage and amount in between are facts or rules. A wrong ``yes'' costs SBB money, a wrong ``no'' costs the passenger money and SBB trust, so whatever the rules cannot settle goes to a person (P7). From this view the domain expert argued for the model as translator (P3) with critique and repair (P6), which the team combined with P1, P4 and P12~\citep{h2refundchat}.

\subsection{Refund policy as rules}
\label{sec:policy}

The domain expert encoded the refund policy of Sheet 2~\citep{h2task} as FrameX rules in \texttt{47-refund-policy.fx}~\citep{h2policy}, on top of the delay that \texttt{46-refunds.fx} measures at the destination from the trains that really ran (Table~\ref{tab:policy}).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Refund policy as derived by the rules. Money is stored in Rappen (integers).}
\label{tab:policy}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Delay at the destination & Derived facts & The reply names \\
\midrule
under 60 min & \texttt{notEntitled}, 0\,\% & the delay and the 60\,min threshold \\
60 to 119 min & \texttt{entitled}, 25\,\% & the delay and 25\,\% \\
120 min or more & \texttt{entitled}, 50\,\% & the delay and 50\,\% \\
amount under CHF~5 & \texttt{entitled}, \texttt{paidOut} false & the CHF~5 minimum payout \\
not measured, or day not in data & \texttt{handOff} with reason & the reason; customer service takes over \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Three choices follow from the domain. Every number of the policy lives in one fact, \texttt{refundPolicy}, so each proof shows the threshold it used and no threshold appears in a prompt or in Python. ``Entitled but not paid out'' is kept apart from ``not entitled'': a passenger with CHF~4.90 due has a claim that only the minimum blocks. And the slot \texttt{dayOnRecord} is closed, so a journey on a day outside the data is handed on, while the rest of the knowledge base stays open-world. Twelve tests on constructed journeys cover the band edges (59/60 and 119/120 minutes), a missing price, a refund under CHF~5, an unmeasured arrival and a day outside the data; all 19 tests in \texttt{tests/test\_refunds.py} pass.

\subsection{Language contract}
\label{sec:contract}

The contract in \texttt{docs/refund-chat.md} fixes what the model may return~\citep{h2refundchat}. Each slot (origin, destination, date, time, price, train) carries the passenger's own words, and the host accepts it only if the quote occurs in the message (P1). The prompt states the format and the meaning of each field, but no threshold, percentage, station list or delay arithmetic. Seven fixed critique reasons, such as \texttt{ambiguous\_station} (``Zürich'' matches 31 stations), go back to the model for one repair round, then to the passenger (P6). For each outcome the contract also lists what the reply must never say, for example ``you get CHF~4.90'' below the minimum, or a verdict in a hand-off. Policy, tests and contract were committed one hour into the open work, so the other roles built on one spec.

\subsection{Checking the spec against the data}
\label{sec:datacheck}

The domain expert then checked the 7 November data against the policy. The largest arrival delay was 71 minutes; of 56,675 recorded arrivals, two were 60 or more minutes late and none 120 or more, so a 50\,\% refund arises only through a cancellation or a missed connection. The task's own example matched four departures within 30 minutes, all checked ones on time: the assistant must ask which train was meant, and the answer is no refund. The domain expert also built the chat with a live workflow graph that shows, for each model call and rule step, what was sent and what came back.
